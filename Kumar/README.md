# Kumar Dataset

The Kumar dataset is a publicly available histopathology nuclei segmentation dataset containing images from multiple tissue types with substantial variations in nuclear morphology and staining appearance.

It was used in our study to evaluate the robustness and generalization ability of the proposed nuclei segmentation framework.

## Usage in Our Study

The Kumar dataset was used for:

- nuclei segmentation evaluation;
- cross-domain adaptation experiments;
- analysis of robustness under heterogeneous staining and tissue conditions.

The predefined experimental partitions used in our study were kept fixed, and test images were excluded from model optimization.
